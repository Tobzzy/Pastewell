Frequently asked questions

Why does macOS show a security warning?＋

The free community build is ad-hoc signed rather than notarized by Apple. After trying to open it once, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Security and choose Open Anyway. Never disable Gatekeeper globally.

Where is my clipboard history stored?＋

Pastewell stores its history only in your user Application Support folder on this Mac. It does not upload or sync it.

Does Pastewell record passwords?＋

Pastewell ignores common concealed and transient clipboard formats used by password managers. You can also pause recording whenever you want.

Which Macs are supported?＋

Pastewell supports macOS Sonoma 14 or newer on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

Is Pastewell really free?＋

Yes. Pastewell is free and open source under the MIT license. The direct community download does not require an account or payment.
